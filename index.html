How to Get Your School ID - First Year

Step 1: Go to Registry / Student Affairs Office with Admission Letter
Step 2: Carry: Admission Letter, National ID/Birth Cert, 2 Passport Photos
Step 3: Fill ID Application Form at the office (Issued at the office)
Step 4: Pay at OFFICIAL Mpesa number 0788245106 . Keep receipt.
Step 5: Return form + receipt to ID office for processing and photo capture
